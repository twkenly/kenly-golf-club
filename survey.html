FAQs

How far is St. George from Vegas?

About 120 miles, or roughly 2 hours on I-15. Utah is one hour ahead of Nevada, so plan Sunday flights with that in mind.

Why fly into Vegas instead of St. George?

LAS has nonstops from NYC, Boston, Cleveland, Denver and Seattle at good prices. St. George's airport is small, with few direct flights. Flying into Vegas also gives us the Vegas night.

What would we do in Vegas?

Whatever the group wants: a concert, a residency or show, or just a casino night. We'll choose once the weekend is set and we can see what's playing.

Which courses would we play?

Two of the three: Black Desert, Sand Hollow and Wolf Creek. Wolf Creek or Black Desert fits on the drive in, and Sand Hollow is a few minutes from the house. Use the cost picker above to compare combinations.

Why is Black Desert $450?

That's its 2026 non-resort-guest rate from March 13 to July 6. It includes cart, a forecaddie, and food and soft drinks during the round. A forecaddie tip is customary and isn't in the estimate.

Does everyone get their own room?

Yes. The Penthouse has 6 bedrooms: two with a king, three with two queens each, and one with two twins.

What's the weather like?

Spring is prime season, with highs mostly in the 70s and 80s. By mid-May it can reach the 90s, so morning tee times are best.

Do we need cars?

Yes. Two rental cars, or one large SUV with room for six sets of clubs, from LAS. This isn't included in the estimate.

FAQs

Why is TPC Scottsdale so expensive?

Dynamic pricing at its peak. In the weeks after the Phoenix Open, morning times reached $550 and the lowest rate was $436 (2026). Prices fall in May. It's optional; We-Ko-Pa and Troon North are better value.

What do Saguaro and Cholla cost?

The same for either course: $329 from Feb 15 to Apr 25, 2027 and $239 from Apr 26 to May 16, 2027, plus tax. We-Ko-Pa doesn't use dynamic pricing, so these won't jump the way TPC does.

Does everyone get their own room?

Not quite. The house has 5 bedrooms and 12 beds, so everyone gets a bed and one room is shared.

How far is Old Town?

About 30 minutes from the house by rideshare. Most nights we'd be at the house, with one Old Town night.

Is spring training happening?

Cactus League games usually run through late March, so a game is only an option on the March weekends.

What's the weather like?

March and April are ideal, with highs in the 70s and 80s. By mid-May afternoons reach the 90s.

Do we need cars?

One large SUV or two cars from PHX for golf and groceries. Rideshare for Old Town. Not included in the estimate.

FAQs

What exactly is in the $2,699?

2 nights room only at the Inn at Spanish Bay, one round at Pebble Beach Golf Links and one at Spyglass Hill, plus all taxes and resort fees. The price is per person with two sharing a room.

Why book through Golfbreaks?

Tee times at Pebble are only guaranteed with a resort stay. The package locks in both rounds and the rooms together, so it's the simplest option to plan.

Will the price change?

Yes, by date. Golfbreaks lists this package from $2,139 on some dates. We'd confirm the price once we pick a weekend.

Does everyone get a bed?

Yes. We'd book three rooms with two queen beds each. Golfbreaks calls these "twin" rooms, which just means two beds.

What isn't included?

Flights, a rental car, meals and drinks, and caddie or forecaddie fees and tips. Expect to pay a caddie or forecaddie at Pebble on top of the package.

Which airport?

SFO has the most nonstops from our home cities and is about 2 hours away. SJC is about 75 minutes but has fewer flights. Monterey (MRY) is closest but has very few flights.
