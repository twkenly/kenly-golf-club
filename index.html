Logistics

Good to know

What's the deal with time zones?

Las Vegas and Mesquite are on Pacific time. St. George and Sand Hollow are on Mountain time, an hour ahead. You lose an hour driving out Friday and get it back Sunday.

How far is everything?

The Strip to Wolf Creek is about 1 hour 15 minutes. Wolf Creek to the house is about 45 minutes. From the house, Black Desert is about 25 minutes, Sand Hollow about 20, and downtown St. George about 15. The house back to LAS is about 2 hours.

What should I pack for golf?

Wolf Creek doesn't allow denim of any kind. Mid-May highs can reach the 90s, so bring sunscreen, a hat and a water bottle. Carts are included at both courses.

What are the house rules?

Quiet hours are 11 PM to 7 AM. No parties, and no smoking or vaping. There are security cameras outside. Cars go in the garage and driveway. Check-in and check-out times come with the booking.

Do we need cars?

We'd get two rental cars or one large SUV at LAS with room for six sets of clubs.

When does booking start?

Once everyone gives the plan a thumbs up in the group text, by Friday, Nov 13. The rental house, Santana tickets and Wolf Creek come first. Sand Hollow tee times can be booked starting Monday, Nov 16, so we'll lock those in as the backup. Black Desert opens 14 days out, around May 1.
