Logistics

Good to know

What's the deal with time zones?

Las Vegas and Mesquite are on Pacific time. St. George and Sand Hollow are on Mountain time, an hour ahead. You lose an hour driving out Friday and get it back Sunday. Every time on this page says which zone it's in.

How far is everything?

The Strip to Wolf Creek is about 1 hour 15 minutes. Wolf Creek to the house is about 50 minutes. Black Desert is about 30 minutes from the house. The house back to LAS is about 2 hours.

What should I pack for golf?

Wolf Creek doesn't allow denim of any kind. Mid-May highs can reach the 90s, so bring sunscreen, a hat and a water bottle. Carts are included at both courses.

Do I need a car?

We'll get two rental cars or one large SUV at LAS with room for six sets of clubs. Coordinate in the group chat once flights are booked.

What if my plans change?

Wolf Creek tee times can be changed for free up until 48 hours before. Tell Tyler as early as you can so the group sizes can be adjusted; no-shows are charged full rate.
